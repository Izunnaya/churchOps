Foundations
Design Language
A calm warm-grey base carries every screen. The four crest colors appear only as small accents — status chips, the primary button, chart fills — never as surface colors.
IBM Plex Sans with tabular numerals for money. Base body 17px on device, inputs 18px, touch targets ≥48px for users aged 50+.
Status is always icon + label, never color alone.

01 · Treasurer
Offering Entry
Count the cash, then split into categories — the same order as the paper form. Every total is computed; the treasurer never types one.
Transfers sit inside categories, so cash and category totals legitimately differ. A calm info strip explains this — it is never styled as an error.
Finalizing states its consequence in plain words and repeats the figures being locked.
